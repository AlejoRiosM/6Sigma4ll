# 6Sigma4ll - Six Sigma Made Simple

Welcome to this project about **Six Sigma**.

This repository is designed to explain Six Sigma in easy words, even if you have no background in statistics, quality, or process improvement.

## What is Six Sigma?

Six Sigma is a method used to improve processes, reduce mistakes, and make work more efficient.

In simple terms, it helps people answer questions like:

- Why are errors happening?
- Where is the process slowing down?
- How can we make results more consistent?
- What changes will improve quality?

The main idea is to use data, not guesses, to make better decisions.

## Why Six Sigma matters

Six Sigma helps organizations:

- Reduce defects and errors.
- Save time and money.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Make processes more stable and predictable.
- Solve problems in a structured way.

## Six Sigma belts

Six Sigma uses belt colors to show different levels of knowledge and responsibility.

### White Belt
- Basic introduction to Six Sigma.
- Understands simple concepts and vocabulary.
- Usually supports improvement projects.

### Yellow Belt
- Knows the basic tools and ideas.
- Can help collect data and identify problems.
- Often participates in team projects.

### Green Belt
- Can work on improvement projects more actively.
- Uses data analysis and problem-solving tools.
- Often supports or leads smaller projects.

### Black Belt
- Deep knowledge of Six Sigma methods.
- Leads complex improvement projects.
- Works with advanced data analysis and statistics.
- Trains and supports Green Belts and other team members.

### Master Black Belt
- Highest technical level in Six Sigma.
- Coaches Black Belts and helps guide the organization.
- Focuses on strategy, training, and large-scale improvement.

### Champion
- Not always considered a belt, but very important.
- Usually a leader or manager who supports the project.
- Removes barriers and helps the team succeed.
